# Contributor Covenant Code of Conduct

This project adopts the Contributor Covenant, version 2.1, available at https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct.html.

The Contributor Covenant is intended to foster a respectful, welcoming, and inclusive community. We expect everyone in the project to follow its principles and to treat one another with professionalism and compassion.

## Our pledge

We as members, contributors, and maintainers pledge to make participation in our community a harassment-free experience for everyone, regardless of age, body size, visible or invisible disability, ethnicity, sex characteristics, gender identity and expression, level of experience, education, socio-economic status, nationality, personal appearance, race, religion, or sexual identity and orientation.

## Our standards

Examples of behavior that contributes to a positive environment include:

- being respectful and considerate in all interactions;
- welcoming different viewpoints and experiences;
- giving and receiving constructive feedback graciously;
- accepting responsibility and apologizing to those affected by mistakes;
- focusing on what is best for the community and the project.

Examples of unacceptable behavior include:

- harassment, intimidation, or abusive conduct;
- discriminatory, insulting, or demeaning comments;
- sexual attention or advances that are unwelcome;
- personal attacks, trolling, or repeated disruption of discussions;
- publishing private information without permission;
- threats of violence or retaliation;
- any behavior intended to silence or isolate others from the community.

## Reporting violations

If you experience or witness behavior that violates this code of conduct, please report it privately to the project maintainers through the repository's supported contact channel for project moderation or security concerns. In GitHub-hosted projects, this usually means the private maintainer contact or moderation route configured for the repository; if such a route is not configured yet, the maintainers should add one before relying on this document as the primary reporting path.

When reporting, include:

- the nature of the incident;
- when and where it occurred;
- any relevant links, screenshots, or supporting context;
- whether there are any immediate safety concerns.

Reports will be handled with discretion and in a manner that protects the reporter as much as practical.

## Enforcement responsibilities

Project maintainers are responsible for clarifying and enforcing this code of conduct and may take appropriate and fair action in response to any reported behavior that they determine violates it.

Enforcement decisions will be aimed at protecting the community, maintaining a productive environment, and being proportionate to the reported behavior. The maintainers may work privately with the involved parties to resolve issues before applying stronger measures.

## Enforcement actions

Possible consequences for unacceptable behavior include, in order of severity:

- a warning or request to stop the behavior;
- a temporary restriction from participation in the project;
- removal from project discussions, events, or contribution spaces;
- permanent exclusion from the project.

The maintainers will consider the context, severity, and patterns of behavior before deciding on a response. The process is intended to be fair, consistent, and focused on the safety and health of the community.

## Scope

This code of conduct applies to all project spaces and interactions, including issue trackers, discussions, pull requests, chat or forum spaces used for the project, and any project-related events.
