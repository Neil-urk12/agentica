A visual workspace OS for agents in development

Give your agents a place to think.

Agentica is an infinite canvas for the messy middle of agent work. Notes, sketches, and instructions live on one surface — instead of scrolling away in a chat log.

01 The surface

Chat logs are a transcript.
This is the desk.

An agent’s work is rarely a straight line. Agentica gives it a place to spread out: artefacts as objects, placement as memory, and the whole board saved exactly where you left it.

01 / space

A canvas that remembers

Pan, zoom, and every node’s position persist with the canvas. Reopening a workspace puts the thinking back on the desk, not at the top of a feed.

02 / instructions

Instructions are objects

Pin a note beside the thing it applies to. It stays put as reference, instead of disappearing upstream in a thread.

03 / storage

Your disk, not our cloud

Each workspace is a SQLite database on your machine, with one writer per workspace. No account, no sync service, nothing to sign up for.

$ ls ~/.local/share/agentica

02 How it works

Three moves, then stay a while.

  1. 01

    Open a workspace

    Name it, and a local SQLite file is created. One workspace, one writer, no daemon in the middle.

    workspace / new
  2. 02

    Put it on the surface

    Write a note, draw a flow, pin an instruction. Arrange it the way you’d arrange paper on a desk.

    text · draw · arrow
  3. 03

    Pick it up later

    Autosave keeps the canvas and its viewport. Close the app, reopen it, and the board is still there.

    saved · just now
